How it works
  1. Tap a player. Save their current photo (the one the apps hold today).
  2. In ChatGPT: attach that photo and paste the style prompt. Several at once is fine: one message per player keeps their faces apart.
  3. Save what ChatGPT makes, back here tap Upload Heist photo, slide the crop if the head is cut, and save. It is cut to 4:5, 1080×1350, the size every Heist card, avatar and story uses.
  4. Many at once: name each file after the player (Munther Rahbi.png) and use Upload a batch. A name two players share is refused; use the id shown on their card instead.

Or give ChatGPT the whole list in one go: the brief holds the prompt, every player still to do, their photo link and the file name to save each one as.
