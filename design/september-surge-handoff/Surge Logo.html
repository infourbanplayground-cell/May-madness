TWO CANDIDATES
Cyan mark #00E5FF
Fully committed to the volume. The mark reads as part of Surge rather than a guest in it, and the whole screen holds one temperature. Costs you the club's only warm note.
Amber mark #FF9E1B
Keeps the club warm while the season goes cool, which is closer to how the orange behaves today. It also spends the accent that Surge reserves for live states — every screen would carry an amber mark that means nothing.
I'D TAKE THE CYAN
Strike Amber is doing a job in this system — it is the one thing on a screen that means happening now. Spending it on a mark that appears in the header of every screen, permanently, drains it. The club identity travels across volumes by its shape, which is untouched here; the colour is the volume's to set.

The app embeds this logo as base64 in a single constant, UP_LOGO_FULL — swapping volumes means replacing that one string. The glow is CSS, not baked into the PNG, so it can be tuned without re-exporting. The source file carried a lot of transparent padding — about two thirds of its width was empty, which is why the app compensates by rendering it at 64px tall. These are trimmed to the artwork plus a 6% margin, so a height value now means what it says. The padded original is kept as a fourth file if you want a like-for-like swap without touching any CSS.
