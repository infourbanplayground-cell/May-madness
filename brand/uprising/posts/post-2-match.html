UPRISING How a match works

Win 11 rallies
and it is over

Every rally you win is one point. No 15-30-40, no deuce, no games, no sets. First pair to 11 wins and the match stops there — so a score is always 11 and whatever the other pair got.

11–7
Around 17 rallies in all. Seven or eight minutes. Then you walk to your next court.
Two things are called points. They are not the same.
In the match 0–11 Rallies you won. Decides who wins this match.
On the table 2–8 What that result pays you. Decides who wins the night.

Win 11–7 on Court 2 and you take 6 points — not 11. The 11 only decided the match. Highest total after 12 rounds wins; there is no number to reach.
