# September Surge — Vol.7 season recap

`surge-recap.mp4`, 1080x1920, 38s. Built by:

```bash
python3 build-recap-video.py     # reads the archived final state + /tmp/race.json
python3 build-recap-audio.py
node record-recap-video.mjs
```

## The story it tells, and where it comes from

Nothing here is typed in. Every figure is computed from
`archive/september-surge/final-state.json` with the app's own scoring engine, so
the video cannot disagree with surge.urbanpadel.om.

| Beat | Fact |
|---|---|
| The numbers | 8 nights, 193 decided matches, 60 players |
| Five nights, one name | Munther Rahbi led from night 2 through night 6 |
| The double-points night | Hamed Amri scored **+62** on session 7 — the biggest single night of the season — and took the lead by 2 |
| One night left | 2 points in it |
| The podium | Hamed Amri 189, Munther Rahbi 188, Nooh Amri 171 |
| The margin | **1 point** across eight nights |
| Next | the lights go out on Vol.7 and come up on Blackout |

## The handover

The recap ends on half a second of black and near-silence — the audio drops 23dB
and comes back — before the Blackout mark snaps in and the palette changes from
Vol.7's cyan to Vol.8's lime. The gap IS the transition; a bed playing through it
would turn the point of the cut into a glitch.

Two more facts that did not make the cut but are true: eight different pairs won
the eight nights (nobody repeated), and twelve different people won one.

## Photographs

under a scrim. They already carry "1ST PLACE" etc. in their own lettering, which
is why the video adds no place kicker of its own — a second one lands straight on
